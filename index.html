Our Story

Made with Passion

Teju Catering & Event is a trusted catering and event management service based in the heart of Thirumangalam, Chennai. With a passion for great food and flawless celebrations, we specialize in delivering delicious, freshly prepared meals and comprehensive event solutions for weddings, corporate gatherings, birthday parties, and all kinds of special occasions. Our team is dedicated to making every event memorable, handling every detail with care and professionalism.

Located at Pillaiyar Koil Street, we proudly serve families and organizations across Chennai with round-the-clock availability, ensuring that no matter when your event is, we are ready to serve. From intimate family functions to large-scale celebrations, Teju Catering & Event combines traditional Tamil flavors with modern presentation to create an unforgettable dining experience for your guests.
